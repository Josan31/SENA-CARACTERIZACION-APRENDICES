# 🎨 SENA Brand Visual Identity Guidelines

This document outlines the visual identity specifications, design tokens, typography rules, and logo usage guidelines for SENA digital applications.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

### Primary / Institutional Color

- **SENA Institutional Green:** `#39A900`
  - **RGB:** `57, 169, 0`
  - **CMYK:** `C: 75% | M: 0% | Y: 100% | K: 0%`
  - **Pantone:** `361 C`
  - _Usage:_ Primary brand color, logo, featured elements, and main Calls to Action (CTA).

### Secondary Colors

- **Dark Green:** `#007832`
  - **RGB:** `0, 120, 50`
  - **CMYK:** `C: 88% | M: 27% | Y: 100% | K: 14%`
- **Dark / Navy Blue:** `#00304D`
  - **RGB:** `0, 48, 77`
  - **CMYK:** `C: 100% | M: 77% | Y: 43% | K: 42%`

### Color Architecture / Complementary Colors

- **Light Blue (Cyan):** `#50E5F9`
  - **RGB:** `80, 229, 249`
  - **CMYK:** `C: 55% | M: 0% | Y: 11% | K: 0%`
- **Yellow / Gold:** `#FDC300`
  - **RGB:** `253, 195, 0`
  - **CMYK:** `C: 0% | M: 25% | Y: 94% | K: 0%`
- **Purple:** `#71277A`
  - **RGB:** `113, 39, 122`
  - **CMYK:** `C: 68% | M: 98% | Y: 11% | K: 2%`

### Neutral Colors & Backgrounds

- **Absolute White:** `#FFFFFF` (`RGB: 255, 255, 255`)
- **Off-White / Light Neutral Background:** `#F6F6F6` (`RGB: 246, 246, 246`)
- **Black:** `#000000` (`RGB: 0, 0, 0`)

## 🔤 Typography & Font Families

### Primary Typography: Work Sans

_Usage:_ Headings, UI components, buttons, and primary content.

> ⚠️ **Accessibility Note:** Thin and Extralight weights **must not** be used in digital interfaces to guarantee legibility and contrast compliance.

#### Allowed Variations:

- Thin / Thin Italic _(Avoid in digital UI)_
- Extralight / Extralight Italic _(Avoid in digital UI)_
- Light / Light Italic
- Regular / Italic
- Medium / Medium Italic
- SemiBold / SemiBold Italic
- Bold / Bold Italic
- Extrabold / Extrabold Italic
- Black / Black Italic

### Secondary Typography: Calibri

_Usage:_ Body copy, internal newsletters, secondary documentation, and informational materials.

#### Allowed Variations:

- Light / Light Italic
- Regular / Italic
- Bold / Bold Italic

## 📐 Logo & Minimum Sizing Rules

- **Digital Minimum Size:** `50px` width (applies to both full logo with text and icon mark).
- **Print Minimum Size:** `1 cm`.
